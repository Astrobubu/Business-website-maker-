## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR SENDING

**Email Best Practices:**
- ✅ Send Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm Dubai time
- ✅ Personalize the first line (mention their location/dish)
- ✅ Keep under 150 words
- ✅ One clear CTA (call to action)
- ✅ Mobile-friendly (most open on phones)

**WhatsApp Best Practices:**
- ✅ Under 100 words
- ✅ Send 9am-11am or 3pm-5pm
- ✅ Use emojis (but not too many)
- ✅ End with a question
- ✅ Wait for reply before sending link spam

**SMS Best Practices:**
- ✅ Under 160 characters if possible
- ✅ Include your name
- ✅ One link maximum
- ✅ Send 10am-6pm only
